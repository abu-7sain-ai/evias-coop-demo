## Metrics (`src/lib/metrics.ts`)
- `same_phone_repeats` counts the most repeated phone on a cashier's invoices **with line voids**. Over all invoices, regular customers reach 16 visits and the planted pattern disappears.
- Queue model: people waiting = Σ traffic(POS-01…06) × 0.057; per lane = ÷ open lanes. Gives 8.0 / 8.1 / 7.0 at Thu 19/20/21 with 6 lanes and ≈ 6 with 8 (Q7).
- Rent KPI = rent collected within the month it fell due ÷ rent due that month; current = October, previous = September (Q4).
- Attendance rate counts every scheduled day, including employees with no records at all — so zero-punch employees lower the rate.
- "Expiring within 14 d" delta compares with the same snapshot taken 7 days earlier (lots received before then).
- Sales per metre: each product's 30-day branch revenue is split across its shelf locations by facings share, then ÷ fixture length (Q6 definition, made fair for products on several shelves).
- Stagnant = days since the product's last actual sale at the branch (> 60), not the generator's `stagnant_days` field — so the screens and the agent read the same sales.
- Lot actions: complaints → hold; stagnant → return; surplus ≤ 3 days → buy-1-get-1; ≤ 7 → discount; ≤ 30 → transfer.
- Rent escalation stages: ≤ 30 days reminder, 31–59 notice ("إنذار"), ≥ 60 ministry — matches story #9.
- Ministry comparison uses the last 90 days. The highlighted "worst" co-op needs ratio > 1.3 (or < 0.85 where higher is better).
- Seasonal forecast assumes 30 Ramadan days; draft PO = demand × last-year multiplier × 30 − current stock.
